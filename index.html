Team Member — MIST Blitz Formula SAE Team

April 2024 – August 2026

Served as a member of the Chassis and Aerodynamics, Logistics Operations, and Finance teams within MIST Blitz Formula SAE, the university's Formula Student program. Designed the team's first Formula Student spaceframe chassis and validated its structural integrity through SolidWorks modeling and ANSYS simulation, iterating on the design to optimize stiffness-to-weight performance. Supervised fabrication to ensure dimensional accuracy and full compliance with Formula Student regulations, and verified critical safety systems including the headrest and mounting assembly.
