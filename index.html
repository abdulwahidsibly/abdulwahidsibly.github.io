PROJECT 05

Laminar Flow Analysis in a Circular Pipe

Analyzed laminar flow through a circular pipe using ANSYS Fluent and compared the numerical results with analytical solutions. The study included geometry development, structured mesh generation, boundary conditions, convergence monitoring, grid-independence analysis and velocity/pressure distribution evaluation.
